## 1. Pilot Overview

### Objectives
The Phase 1 pilot aims to validate the core hypothesis: **A safe, parent-controlled voice journaling app can help young cricket players (ages 9-14) build self-reflection habits and emotional awareness, while providing parents with meaningful insights.**

### Pilot Scope
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Participants:** 5-10 parent-child pairs from 1-2 cricket academies
- **Target Engagement:** 4+ check-ins per child per week
- **Geographic Focus:** Single city/region (simplify support and in-person touchpoints)

### Pilot Goals
1. **Validate product-market fit:** Do kids use the app consistently?
2. **Test safety systems:** Are safety rules catching concerning content appropriately?
3. **Measure parent trust:** Do parents feel confident in safety controls?
4. **Gather qualitative feedback:** What works? What doesn't?
5. **Identify blockers:** Technical issues, UX friction, trust barriers

---

## 2. Academy Selection Criteria

### Ideal Academy Profile
- **Size:** 50-200 active students (large enough for sample, small enough for personal relationships)
- **Age range:** Majority of students are 9-14 years old
- **Parent engagement:** Academy has active parent communication (WhatsApp groups, newsletters, etc.)
- **Tech-friendly:** Parents and kids have smartphones/tablets with internet access
- **Trust relationship:** Academy owner/coach is open to innovation and has strong parent relationships
- **Location:** Easily accessible for in-person support during pilot

### Red Flags (Avoid These Academies)
- ❌ High-pressure, competition-only focus (may create toxic metrics culture)
- ❌ Poor parent communication (hard to onboard and gather feedback)
- ❌ Low tech adoption (parents/kids unfamiliar with apps)
- ❌ No established safeguarding policies (safety culture mismatch)

### Outreach Strategy
1. **Personal networks first:** Reach out to academies where you have existing relationships
2. **Coach endorsement:** Get buy-in from head coach (they're trusted by parents)
3. **Pilot incentive:** Offer free access + personalized support (no payment required)
4. **Social proof:** Share vision and early prototypes to build excitement

## 3. Pilot Timeline

### Pre-Pilot (Week -2 to Week 0)

#### Week -2: Academy Partnership
- Identify and confirm 1-2 pilot academies
- Sign partnership agreement (data sharing, feedback commitment)
- Schedule in-person kickoff session with academy coaches

#### Week -1: Parent Recruitment
- Academy sends pilot invitation to parents (via WhatsApp/email)
- **Target recruitment:** 10-15 parent-child pairs (expect ~50% dropout)
- Parents complete consent form and pre-pilot survey
- Finalize participant list (5-10 confirmed pairs)

#### Week 0: Onboarding
- In-person or virtual onboarding session with parents and kids (separate sessions)
- Parents create accounts, receive training
- Kids receive Kid Codes, practice first check-in
- Technical support available for setup issues

---

### Pilot Period (Week 1-4)

#### Week 1: Launch & Handholding
- **Goal:** Get every child to complete at least 1 check-in
- Daily check-ins from product team: "How's it going?"
- Quick troubleshooting for technical issues
- Monitor usage: Who's not using it? Why?

#### Week 2: Habit Formation
- **Goal:** Build consistency (3+ check-ins per child this week)
- Send mid-week encouragement: "Great job! Keep it up!"
- Identify and address friction points (e.g., "Recording doesn't work on my phone")
- First parent feedback session: "What's working? What's confusing?"

#### Week 3: Steady State
- **Goal:** Observe natural usage patterns without intervention
- Minimal prompting (let kids/parents use it independently)
- Monitor safety flags: Are they catching real issues? Too many false positives?
- Collect weekly summary feedback from parents

#### Week 4: Feedback & Wrap-Up
- **Goal:** Gather comprehensive feedback and measure outcomes
- Parent exit survey + in-person focus group (if possible)
- Kid feedback session (fun, low-pressure group discussion)
- Analyze usage data: retention, engagement, satisfaction
- Decide: Expand pilot or iterate on Phase 1?

---

### Post-Pilot (Week 5-6)

#### Week 5: Analysis & Iteration
- Synthesize feedback into prioritized improvements
- Identify critical bugs or UX blockers for Phase 2
- Update product roadmap based on learnings

#### Week 6: Next Steps
- If successful: Plan Phase 2 pilot with 3-5 academies
- If needs work: Iterate on Phase 1 and re-pilot with same academy
- Share results with academy partners (transparency builds trust)

## 4. Onboarding Process

### 4.1 Parent Onboarding (60 minutes)

**Format:** In-person or Zoom session (max 15 parents)

**Agenda:**
1. **Introduction (10 min)**
   - Why this app exists: Help kids reflect, build emotional awareness
   - What makes it safe: No public sharing, parent controls, safety rules
   - What we're learning: This is a pilot—your feedback matters

2. **Account Setup (15 min)**
   - Walk through signup process (email, password, consent)
   - Create first child profile
   - Receive Kid Code (emphasize: save it now, won't see again)
   - Test: Parents log in and see empty dashboard

3. **Dashboard Tour (15 min)**
   - How to view check-ins
   - How to play audio and read transcripts
   - How to generate weekly summaries
   - How safety alerts work (reassure: you'll be notified)

4. **Your Role as a Parent (10 min)**
   - This is not spying—it's supporting reflection
   - Encourage kids to check in 3-4 times/week
   - Review check-ins weekly (don't micromanage)
   - Trust the process: patterns emerge over time

5. **Q&A (10 min)**
   - Address concerns (privacy, safety, screen time)
   - Provide support contact: WhatsApp number or email

**Materials Provided:**
- Printed Quick Start Guide (1-page)
- FAQ sheet (common questions)
- Support contact info

---

### 4.2 Kid Onboarding (45 minutes)

**Format:** In-person group session (max 15 kids, ages 9-14)

**Agenda:**
1. **Introduction (10 min)**
   - "This is your private cricket journal—no one else sees it but your parent"
   - Talk about practice, how you feel, what you're proud of
   - A friendly app will respond and encourage you
   - This is NOT about scores or performance—it's about YOU

2. **Demo (10 min)**
   - Product team demonstrates first check-in (use sample kid account)
   - Show: Record voice → Answer quick questions → Get response
   - Emphasize: Takes less than 5 minutes

3. **Hands-On Practice (20 min)**
   - Kids log in using their Kid Code
   - Record first check-in (can be about today's session)
   - Submit and see response
   - Troubleshoot any issues (microphone permissions, etc.)

4. **Guidelines (5 min)**
   - Check in 3-4 times a week (after practice is best)
   - Be honest—there are no wrong answers
   - If you're feeling bad, it's okay to say so (that's what this is for)
   - Your parent can hear your recordings (it's safe with them)

**Materials Provided:**
- Laminated Kid Code card (to keep in cricket bag)
- Sticker or small incentive (non-competitive: "I'm a reflective cricketer!")

### 5.2 Child Assent (Age-Appropriate)

**Verbal assent required during kid onboarding:**

> "Hi [Child Name]! We're trying out a new app that lets you talk about your cricket practice. Your parent said it's okay, but we want to make sure YOU're okay with it too.
>
> Here's what it does:
> - You record a short voice message after practice (like leaving a voicemail)
> - You answer a few quick questions (How do you feel? What did you focus on?)
> - The app sends you a supportive message back
> - Your parent can listen to your recordings
>
> You can stop using it anytime if you don't like it. Does this sound okay to you?"

**Kid responds:** "Yes" → Proceed with onboarding

**If kid says "No":**
- Thank them for being honest
- Inform parent (kid doesn't want to participate)
- Do not pressure or incentivize (respect autonomy)

## 6. Training & Support

### 6.1 Support Channels

**During Pilot:**
- **WhatsApp Support Group:** Parents + product team (for quick questions)
- **Email Support:** support@aistudentsuccess.app (for detailed issues)
- **Office Hours:** 2x per week, 30-min Zoom sessions (open Q&A)

**Response Time SLA:**
- WhatsApp: <2 hours during business hours (9am-6pm local time)
- Email: <24 hours

---

### 6.2 Common Support Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Parent Solution | Kid Solution |
|-------|----------------|--------------|
| Forgot Kid Code | "Go to Child Settings → View Kid Code (requires parent login)" | "Ask your parent—they have it in the app" |
| Microphone not working | "Check browser permissions: Settings → Privacy → Microphone" | "Ask your parent to help fix it" |
| Recording won't upload | "Check internet connection. Try again in a few minutes." | "Try again later or ask your parent" |
| Can't see transcripts | "Click 'Show Transcript' next to each entry" | N/A |
| Safety alert confusion | "Call us! We'll walk you through it." | N/A |

## 8. Data Collection & Feedback

### 8.1 Pre-Pilot Survey (Parents)

**Purpose:** Establish baseline expectations and concerns

**Questions:**
1. How often does your child currently reflect on their cricket practice? (Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Often)
2. What are your biggest concerns about your child's cricket journey? (Open-ended)
3. How comfortable are you with your child using voice recording apps? (1-5 scale)
4. What would make you feel safe about your child using this app? (Open-ended)
5. How do you currently stay informed about your child's emotional well-being? (Open-ended)

---

### 8.2 Mid-Pilot Check-In (Week 2)

**Format:** Short WhatsApp poll or Google Form

**Questions:**
1. Has your child used the app this week? (Yes / No)
2. If no, what's the main blocker? (Too busy / Forgot / Technical issue / Not interested)
3. Have you reviewed any check-ins? (Yes / No)
4. If yes, was it helpful? (Yes / No / Somewhat)
5. Any issues or questions? (Open-ended)

---

### 8.3 Exit Survey (Parents)

**Purpose:** Measure satisfaction and gather improvement ideas

**Questions:**
1. How satisfied are you with the app overall? (1-5 scale)
2. How easy was it to use? (1-5 scale)
3. Did you feel confident about the safety features? (Yes / No / Somewhat)
4. Did the weekly summaries provide useful insights? (Yes / No / Somewhat)
5. What did you like most about the app? (Open-ended)
6. What frustrated you or didn't work well? (Open-ended)
7. Would you recommend this app to other cricket parents? (Yes / No / Maybe)
8. If we charged $5/month, would you pay for it? (Yes / No / Maybe)

---

### 8.4 Exit Feedback (Kids)

**Format:** In-person group discussion (fun, casual, snacks provided)

**Discussion Topics:**
1. "Show of hands: Who liked using the app?"
2. "What was the best part?" (Let kids share openly)
3. "What was annoying or confusing?"
4. "Did the app help you think about your cricket practice differently?"
5. "Would you want to keep using it?"

**Note:** Keep it light and pressure-free. Kids are honest when comfortable.

---

### 8.5 Usage Analytics (Automated)

**Track in Backend:**
- Number of check-ins per child per week
- Average recording duration
- Completion rate (started vs. submitted)
- Safety flags triggered (by category and severity)
- Parent logins per week
- Weekly summaries generated
- Data exports requested
- Account deletions

## 9. Rollout Plan

### Phase 1 Pilot (Current Plan)
- **Academies:** 1-2
- **Participants:** 5-10 parent-child pairs
- **Duration:** 4 weeks
- **Focus:** Validate core value, identify critical issues

---

### Phase 2 Pilot (If Phase 1 Succeeds)

## 10. Risk Management

### 10.1 Technical Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Audio recording fails on iOS Safari | Medium | High | Test extensively; provide fallback (manual text entry) |
| Database corruption | Low | Critical | Daily backups; test restore process before pilot |
| Server downtime during pilot | Medium | High | Use reliable hosting (Replit/Railway); monitor uptime |
| Audio files fill storage | Low | Medium | Set file size limits; plan storage capacity |

---

### 10.2 Safety Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Safety system misses critical keyword | Low | Critical | Conservative keyword list; manual review of all flagged entries |
| False positives overwhelm parents | Medium | Medium | Tune keyword sensitivity; provide severity levels |
| Parent doesn't see safety alert | Medium | High | Prominent dashboard badges; Phase 2: email/SMS alerts |
| Child shares concerning content but is flagged incorrectly | Low | High | Clear parent guidance: "Trust your instincts; safety system is a tool, not replacement for judgment" |

---

### 10.3 Trust & Adoption Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Parents worry about privacy | High | High | Transparent consent process; emphasize parent control |
| Kids feel monitored/uncomfortable | Medium | High | Clear messaging: "This is for reflection, not surveillance"; get kid assent |
| Low engagement (kids don't use it) | Medium | High | Simple UX; in-person onboarding; weekly encouragement |
| Academy withdraws mid-pilot | Low | High | Build strong coach relationship; regular check-ins |

---

### 10.4 Contingency Plans

**If a child discloses abuse in a check-in:**
1. Safety system flags entry immediately
2. Parent sees critical alert on login
3. Product team manually reviews (within 24 hours)
4. If genuine risk, contact parent directly: "We saw a concerning check-in. Have you reviewed it?"
5. Provide crisis resources (CHILDLINE 1098 in India, etc.)
6. Document incident (anonymized) for learnings

**If technical issues block majority of users:**
1. Pause pilot immediately
2. Fix issue in dev environment
3. Test with 2-3 pilot families before re-launching
4. Extend pilot by 1 week to compensate for downtime

**If parent requests data deletion mid-pilot:**
1. Honor request immediately (no questions asked)
2. Follow up: "We're sorry to see you go. Can you share what didn't work?" (optional)
3. Document reason for future improvement
